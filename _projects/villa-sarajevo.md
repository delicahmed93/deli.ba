## Project overview

Villa Sarajevo is conceived as a calm, contemporary residence with a strong relationship between interior living spaces and the garden.

## Design idea

The project organizes public and private zones with a clear architectural language, using generous openings, natural materials, and warm neutral tones.

## Key features

- open-plan ground floor
- private upper-level bedrooms
- integrated outdoor terrace
- clean modern facade
